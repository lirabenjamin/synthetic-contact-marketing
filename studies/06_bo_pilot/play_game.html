Bayesian learner fit to your splits

The model assumes you held a belief about how much more one market pays. You started at a prior mean. After each round you moved toward that round's revenue signal by a learning rate of 1 / (n0 + round). Here n0 is your prior strength in rounds of feedback, and a large n0 means a sticky prior. Each split is the best split for your belief that round, plus choice noise. Evidence weight compares you with an ideal Bayesian for this game, whose prior strength is n0* = 2 / SNR². The ranges are 80% posterior intervals.
